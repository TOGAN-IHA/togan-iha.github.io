Teknofest UAV Engineering

Team Aleris is engineering a highly capable, autonomous platform for the Teknofest UAV competition. Our custom-designed VTOL QuadPlane or traditional Fixed-Wing aircraft is built to meet the strict maximum take-off weight (MTOW) limit of 4 kg, including the payload[cite: 2].

Hardware & Avionics Architecture

Our aircraft integrates a lightweight 3D-printed modular nose for avionics with a carbon fiber and foam core wing structure[cite: 2]. To ensure rapid deployment, the airframe utilizes quick-release wing connectors equipped with integrated Pogo pins for servos[cite: 2].

  • Primary Flight Controller: A Pixhawk flight controller serves as the low-level real-time brain of the aircraft, executing high-speed PID control loops and hardware-level emergencies like automatic fail-safe modes[cite: 2]. It is mounted at the center of gravity on vibration-damping foam[cite: 2].
  • Companion Computer: A Raspberry Pi 5 runs Ubuntu Linux, ROS 2, and OpenCV to process video feeds, recognize ground targets, and send high-level navigational commands to the Pixhawk[cite: 2].
  • Sensors and Telemetry: A downward-facing LiDAR measures the exact distance to the ground, strictly enforcing the 120-meter altitude limit and calculating payload drop trajectories[cite: 2]. A pitot tube measures airspeed to prevent aerodynamic stalls[cite: 2]. A single vision camera is utilized to detect the specific colors and shapes of payload targets on the ground[cite: 2].
  • Emergency Safety Systems: A heavy-duty Solid State Relay (SSR) or MOSFET is wired in series with the main battery, controlled by a physical exterior toggle switch that instantly cuts all power to motors and avionics within a 2-second limit[cite: 2].

Software Stack

  • Operating System & Middleware: The Raspberry Pi 5 operates on Ubuntu Linux (22.04 or 24.04) and utilizes ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2) to provide a distributed, node-based architecture[cite: 2].
  • Flight Stack & Communication: The Pixhawk runs ArduPilot (ArduPlane)[cite: 2]. MAVROS translates ROS 2 topics from the companion computer into MAVLink serial protocol messages that the Pixhawk can understand[cite: 2].
  • Computer Vision: We utilize a highly optimized C++ OpenCV vision pipeline to process camera frames rapidly, ensuring the payload release command is mathematically deterministic and triggered at the exact right millisecond[cite: 2].

Competition Objectives

  • Mission 1: The UAV must be fully assembled and ready to fly within an 8-minute limit[cite: 2]. It takes off autonomously and executes two continuous horizontal figure-8 (∞) maneuvers around reference poles spaced approximately 150 meters apart[cite: 2].
  • Mission 2: The UAV carries two payloads and utilizes a single camera to detect randomly placed ground targets[cite: 2]. A red payload must be dropped onto a 4x4 meter blue square target, and a blue payload onto a 2x2 meter red square target[cite: 2].
